## Shaiful Ridhwan

Official website of Shaiful Ridhwan, a Malaysian writer focusing on free-form poetry, modern literature, philosophy, and reflective writing.

The website and its content are primarily presented in Bahasa Melayu, reflecting the writer's focus on contemporary Malay literary expression.
